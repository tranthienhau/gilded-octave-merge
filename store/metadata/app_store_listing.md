## Description
The house lights dim. Take your place as Maestro and conduct a cascade of
faceted jewels. Same meets same, two become one - Garnet to Citrine, Sapphire to
Diamond - each fusion ringing its note of the scale. Climb all ten tiers and fuse
two Diamonds into the durable Mega Diamond to win the night.

But the tempo quickens. After a calm overture the drops accelerate from Adagio to
Presto, and any jewel resting above the danger line for too long brings the
curtain crashing down. One polished mode, infinitely replayable.

- Ten-tier gem ladder climbing to a single Mega Diamond end-goal
- Real-time physics merge gameplay on an HTML5 canvas
- An adaptive orchestral score that swells with the tension on the board
- A theatrical light-dim overture before every performance
- Earn a +1 life encore with a rewarded video, or revive with Ducats
